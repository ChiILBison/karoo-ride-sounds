# Privacy

- Ride Sounds requests no location, GPS, internet, contacts, account, camera, or microphone permission.
- Media permissions are used only to validate optional `sound.wav` and `custom-icon.png` files in `Download/Karoo Ride Sounds/`.
- Ride and live-segment state is received locally from the Karoo extension interface. The app does not upload it.
- Recent diagnostic entries remain in the app's private storage on the device. They may include timestamps and ride-state decisions, but no route coordinates. Use **Clear local diagnostics** in the app to erase them.
- Uninstalling the app removes private settings and diagnostics. Files that you placed in Downloads remain until you delete them.
